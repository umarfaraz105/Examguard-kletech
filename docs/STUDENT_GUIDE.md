## Getting Started

### What is ExamGuard?

ExamGuard is the examination management system used by your institution to:
- Schedule exams for all courses
- Assign you to specific exam rooms and seats
- Generate your hall tickets
- Publish your grades after exams

As a student, you use ExamGuard to access your personal exam information.

### What You Need

- **Internet connection** (WiFi or mobile data)
- **Web browser** (Chrome, Firefox, Edge, or Safari)
- **Login credentials** provided by your institution
  - Email address
  - Password
- **Printer** (to print hall ticket before exams)

## Logging In

### Step-by-Step Login

1. **Open Your Web Browser**
   - Type the ExamGuard URL in the address bar
   - Typically: `http://examguard.kletu.ac.in` (check with your institution)
   - Or: Use the link provided in your student portal

2. **Select Your Role**
   - On the login page, you'll see a role dropdown
   - Click and select **"Student"**

3. **Enter Your Credentials**
   - **Email Address**: Your institutional email (e.g., student@kletu.ac.in)
   - **Password**: Provided by administration or set during registration

4. **Click "Sign In"**
   - You'll be logged in and redirected to your dashboard

[Screenshot: Login page with Student role selected]

### First-Time Login

If this is your first time logging in:

1. **Use Temporary Password**: You'll receive initial credentials via email
2. **Change Password Immediately**:
   - After login, click on your name in the top-right corner
   - Select "Change Password"
   - Enter current (temporary) password
   - Enter new password (minimum 8 characters)
   - Confirm new password
   - Click "Save"

**Password Requirements:**
- At least 8 characters long
- Include letters and numbers
- Use at least one special character (recommended)
- Don't use easily guessed passwords (like "password123")

[Screenshot: Change password dialog]

### Forgot Password?

If you forget your password:

1. Click **"Forgot Password?"** link on the login page
2. Enter your registered email address
3. Click "Send Reset Link"
4. Check your email for password reset instructions
5. Click the link in the email (valid for 24 hours)
6. Set your new password
7. Log in with new password

**Note**: If you don't receive the reset email within 5 minutes:
- Check your spam/junk folder
- Verify you entered the correct email address
- Contact your institution's exam cell or IT support

## Downloading Your Hall Ticket

### What is a Hall Ticket?

Your hall ticket (also called admit card) is an essential document that includes:
- Your photo and student details
- Complete exam schedule for the cycle (ISA1, ISA2, or ESA)
- Room and seat assignments
- QR code for verification
- Exam rules and instructions

**You must bring your hall ticket to every exam.**

[Screenshot: Sample hall ticket with annotations]

### When Hall Tickets Are Available

Hall tickets become available after:
1. Exam schedule is finalized
2. Seating assignments are completed
3. Exam plan is published by exam cell

You'll receive a notification when your hall ticket is ready to download.

### Downloading Your Hall Ticket

1. **Log in to ExamGuard**
2. **Navigate to Hall Tickets**:
   - Click **"Hall Tickets"** in the sidebar
   - Or: Click **"Download Hall Ticket"** on dashboard
3. **Select Exam Cycle**:
   - Choose the assessment: ISA1, ISA2, or ESA
   - Select academic year if prompted
4. **Click "Download Hall Ticket"**
5. **PDF Downloads**: Hall ticket file saves to your device
6. **Print Your Hall Ticket**: Print on A4 paper

[Screenshot: Hall ticket download page]

### Hall Ticket Information

Your hall ticket includes:

**Personal Details:**
- Your name
- Student ID number
- Department and semester
- Photo (if available in system)

**Exam Details:**
- Complete schedule for that exam cycle
- All courses you're registered for
- Date, time, and session for each exam
- Room numbers and seat/bench assignments

**QR Code:**
- Unique verification code
- Scanned at exam hall for attendance

**Instructions:**
- Exam rules (reporting time, materials allowed, conduct)
- Contact information for queries

### Printing Your Hall Ticket

**Best Practices:**
- Print on white A4 paper
- Use good quality printer (clear text and QR code)
- Print 2 copies: one primary, one backup
- Don't fold or crumple
- Keep in a safe, dry place

**Color vs Black & White:**
- Black and white printing is acceptable
- QR code must be clearly visible

### Lost Hall Ticket

If you lose your hall ticket before exams:

1. **Re-download**: Log in to ExamGuard and download again
2. **Print New Copy**: You can download unlimited times
3. **Exam Day Emergency**: If you can't print before exam:
   - Download PDF to your phone
   - Show digital copy to invigilator
   - Exam cell may verify manually

## Finding Your Seat Assignment

### Why Seating is Assigned

To ensure:
- Fair examination environment
- Adequate spacing between students
- Efficient invigilator supervision
- Prevention of malpractice

Seating is assigned automatically by ExamGuard based on:
- Room capacity
- Number of students per exam
- Institutional seating rules

### When Seating is Available

Seat assignments are finalized after:
1. Exam scheduling is complete
2. Room allocation is done
3. Exam plan is approved and published

**Timeline**: Typically available 3-7 days before first exam.

### Viewing Your Seat Assignment

**Method 1: From Hall Ticket**
- Your hall ticket shows room and seat for each exam
- Print hall ticket - it's your reference during exam

**Method 2: Seating Page**
1. **Navigate to**: Click **"Seating"** in sidebar
2. **Select Exam**: Choose the specific exam or date
3. **View Assignment**: Your room number and bench number are displayed

[Screenshot: Student seating view]

**Method 3: From Schedule**
- In your exam schedule, seating information appears in the row for each exam

### Understanding Seat Information

**Room Number:** 
- Format: LH-101, CR-205, LAB-301
- LH = Lecture Hall, CR = Classroom, LAB = Laboratory
- Number indicates building/floor/room

**Bench Number:**
- Sequential number (1, 2, 3, ...)
- Benches are numbered in order in each room
- Usually labeled physically in the exam room

**Seat Position:** (if applicable)
- Left, Center, Right
- Indicates position on shared bench (for ESA exams)

**Example:**
- Room: LH-101
- Bench: 25
- Position: Left

This means: Go to Lecture Hall 101, find bench number 25, sit on the left side.

[Screenshot: Seating information display]

### Locating Your Exam Room

**Before Exam Day:**
1. **Check Room Location**: Ask seniors or check campus map
2. **Visit the Room**: If possible, locate the room a day before
3. **Note Landmarks**: Nearby buildings, floor, wing

**On Exam Day:**
- Arrive early (30 minutes before start time)
- Look for room direction signs (often posted during exams)
- Ask security or staff if you can't find the room
- Enter the room calmly and find your bench number

**Bench Numbering:**
- Benches are usually numbered from front to back
- Number cards/labels placed on each bench
- If unclear, ask the invigilator politely

Grades are published after:

## Common Student Issues

### Issue: Cannot Log In

**Possible Causes and Solutions:**

1. **Wrong Password**
   - Solution: Try again carefully, check Caps Lock
   - If forgotten: Use "Forgot Password" link

2. **Wrong Email Address**
   - Solution: Use your institutional email exactly as registered
   - Check for typos (e.g., .com vs .ac.in)

3. **Account Not Created**
   - Solution: Contact exam cell or administration
   - You may need to be registered in the system first

4. **Account Locked** (after multiple failed attempts)
   - Solution: Wait 30 minutes and try again
   - Or: Contact IT support to unlock

5. **Wrong Role Selected**
   - Solution: Ensure "Student" role is selected in dropdown

### Issue: Hall Ticket Not Available

**Possible Causes:**

1. **Plan Not Published Yet**
   - Solution: Hall tickets available only after exam plan is published
   - Check dashboard for announcement or contact exam cell

2. **Not Enrolled in Courses**
   - Solution: Verify your course enrollments with exam cell
   - You must be registered for courses to appear in exam plan

3. **Wrong Exam Cycle Selected**
   - Solution: Ensure you're selecting the correct exam cycle (ISA1, ISA2, ESA)

4. **Technical Issue**
   - Solution: Try different browser or clear browser cache
   - Contact IT support if problem persists

### Issue: Seat Assignment Not Showing

**Possible Causes:**

1. **Seating Not Finalized**
   - Solution: Seating is assigned closer to exam dates
   - Check announcements for when seating will be available

2. **Not Included in Published Plan**
   - Solution: Verify course enrollment
   - Contact exam cell if you're registered but not appearing

3. **Hall Ticket Not Generated**
   - Solution: Download hall ticket first (seating included)

### Issue: Grades Not Visible

**Possible Causes:**

1. **Grades Not Published Yet**
   - Solution: Grades are published after evaluation is complete
   - Check for publication announcement

2. **Grade Withheld** (due to pending issues)
   - Solution: Contact exam cell or administration
   - May be due to attendance, fee, or conduct issues

3. **Wrong Semester/Exam Selected**
   - Solution: Check filters on grades page
   - Ensure correct semester and exam type selected

### Issue: Wrong Information in Profile

**Examples:**
- Name spelled incorrectly
- Wrong department or semester
- Wrong student ID

**Solution:**
1. Take screenshot of error
2. Contact exam cell or administration
3. Submit correction request with proof (ID card, admission documents)
4. Admin will update your profile
5. Verify correction after update

### Issue: Exam Schedule Conflicts

If you see two exams at the same time (very rare):

1. **Take Screenshot** of both exams
2. **Immediately Contact Exam Cell**:
   - Email: examcell@kletu.ac.in
   - Visit office in person if urgent
3. **Provide Details**:
   - Your student ID
   - Both course codes
   - Exam date and time showing conflict
4. **Wait for Resolution**: Exam cell will investigate and fix
5. **Check Updated Schedule**: After resolution, verify on ExamGuard

**Note**: The scheduling system (Engine 1) is designed to prevent conflicts, so this should be extremely rare.

## Exam Day Preparation

### Day Before Exam

**Checklist:**
- [ ] Verify exam date, time, and session in your schedule
- [ ] Print hall ticket (if not done already)
- [ ] Check room number and bench number
- [ ] Locate exam room on campus (if unfamiliar)
- [ ] Prepare materials:
  - [ ] Hall ticket (2 copies recommended)
  - [ ] Student ID card
  - [ ] Pens (blue/black ink, multiple)
  - [ ] Pencils, eraser (for diagrams/sketches)
  - [ ] Calculator (if allowed for that exam)
  - [ ] Water bottle (transparent, label removed)
- [ ] Review allowed materials list for that exam
- [ ] Set multiple alarms to wake up on time

[Screenshot: Exam checklist visual]

### Exam Day Morning/Afternoon

**Timing:**
- **Forenoon Exam** (10 AM start): Arrive by 9:30 AM
- **Afternoon Exam** (2 PM start): Arrive by 1:30 PM

**Before Leaving:**
- [ ] Hall ticket (check again!)
- [ ] Student ID card
- [ ] Stationery
- [ ] Wear comfortable clothing
- [ ] Eat a light meal (don't go hungry)
- [ ] Use restroom before leaving

### Arriving at Exam Hall

1. **Go to Your Assigned Room**: Check hall ticket for room number
2. **Find Your Bench**: Look for your bench number
3. **Sit Down Quietly**: Do not disturb others
4. **Place Hall Ticket on Desk**: Keep visible for invigilator verification
5. **Wait for Instructions**: Invigilator will give instructions
6. **Mobile Phones OFF**: Switch off and keep in bag (not on desk)

### During the Exam

**Rules to Follow:**
- **Silence**: No talking to other students
- **No Cheating**: Keep eyes on your own paper
- **Allowed Materials Only**: Don't bring prohibited items
- **Raise Hand**: If you need help or have a question for invigilator
- **Restroom**: Raise hand and ask permission (usually not allowed in first/last 30 min)
- **Time Management**: Keep track of time yourself

### After the Exam

- **Stay Seated**: Until invigilator collects all answer sheets
- **Submit Answer Sheet**: Hand to invigilator or place as instructed
- **Take Your Belongings**: Hall ticket, stationery, water bottle
- **Leave Quietly**: Don't disturb others still writing

## Frequently Asked Questions

### General Questions

**Q: How do I get my login credentials?**
A: Your email and initial password are provided by your institution's administration or exam cell, usually at the time of admission or course registration.

**Q: Can I use ExamGuard on my mobile phone?**
A: Yes! ExamGuard is mobile-friendly. You can access it on any device with a web browser and internet connection.

**Q: Is my data secure on ExamGuard?**
A: Yes. ExamGuard uses encryption and secure authentication. Your password is encrypted, and access is logged for security.

**Q: Can I access ExamGuard from outside campus?**
A: Yes, if your institution allows it. ExamGuard is typically accessible from anywhere with an internet connection. Check with your IT department if you have issues.

### Exam Schedule Questions

**Q: When will my exam schedule be available?**
A: Exam schedules are typically published 2-4 weeks before the first exam. You'll receive a notification when it's ready.

**Q: Can I request a change in my exam schedule?**
A: Exam dates and times are generally fixed. Only in exceptional circumstances (medical emergency, bereavement) may the institution allow special arrangements. Contact your exam cell with documentation.

**Q: What if I have two exams very close together?**
A: The system ensures minimum gap days (e.g., 1-2 days) between exams for the same student. If you feel the gap is too short, it's within institutional policy. Prepare accordingly.

**Q: What do Forenoon and Afternoon sessions mean?**
A: 
- **Forenoon**: Morning session, typically 10:00 AM to 1:00 PM
- **Afternoon**: After lunch, typically 2:00 PM to 5:00 PM

### Hall Ticket Questions

**Q: Can I download my hall ticket multiple times?**
A: Yes! Download and print as many copies as you need. The PDF is always available until the exam cycle ends.

**Q: What if my photo is not on the hall ticket?**
A: If your photo was not uploaded in the system, the hall ticket may not have it. Bring your student ID card with photo to the exam. Contact administration to upload your photo for future exams.

**Q: Can I use a digital hall ticket (on phone) instead of printed?**
A: Policies vary by institution. Generally, a printed hall ticket is preferred. Check your institutional guidelines. If printer not available, explain to invigilator on exam day.

**Q: What if my name is spelled wrong on hall ticket?**
A: Contact exam cell immediately with correct spelling and proof (ID card). They'll update the system. You may be allowed to write exam with incorrect hall ticket if you have valid ID, but get it corrected soon.

### Seating Questions

**Q: Can I choose my own seat?**
A: No, seating is assigned automatically by the system to ensure fairness and prevent malpractice.

**Q: What if I don't like my seat assignment (back bench, next to certain person)?**
A: Seating assignments are final and cannot be changed based on preference. All seats are supervised equally during exams.

**Q: What if I can't find my bench number?**
A: Arrive early and ask the invigilator. Benches are usually numbered clearly. Don't panic; invigilators will help you.

**Q: Can two students sit on the same bench?**
A: It depends on the exam type and institutional rules:
- **ISA exams**: Usually 1 student per bench (strict spacing)
- **ESA exams**: May allow 2-3 students per bench
Your assignment will specify if there's a position (left/right).

### Grades Questions

**Q: When will my grades be available?**
A: Typically 2-4 weeks after the exam, but varies by institution and course. Check for announcements.

**Q: Why can't I see my grade for one course?**
A: Possible reasons:
- Grade not yet entered by faculty
- Grade withheld due to pending issue (check with exam cell)
- Revaluation in progress
- Technical error (contact IT support)

**Q: Can I request revaluation/rechecking?**
A: If your institution has a revaluation policy, ExamGuard may have a "Revaluation" section. Check there, or follow your institution's offline process.

**Q: What if my grade seems wrong?**
A: First, verify the marks and course. If you believe there's an error, contact your course faculty or submit a revaluation request per institutional policy.

### Technical Questions

**Q: Which browsers are supported?**
A: ExamGuard works best on:
- Google Chrome (recommended)
- Mozilla Firefox
- Microsoft Edge
- Safari (for Mac users)
Avoid Internet Explorer (outdated).

**Q: The website is loading slowly. What should I do?**
A: 
- Check your internet connection
- Try refreshing the page (F5)
- Close other browser tabs
- Clear browser cache
- Try during off-peak hours (evening/night) if many students are accessing

**Q: I'm getting an error message. What should I do?**
A: 
- Take a screenshot of the exact error
- Note what you were trying to do
- Try logging out and logging in again
- Clear browser cache and cookies
- Try a different browser
- Contact IT support with screenshot if problem persists

**Q: Can I use ExamGuard offline?**
A: No, ExamGuard requires an internet connection to access live data. Download your schedule and hall ticket for offline reference.

### Contact and Support

**Q: Who do I contact for help?**
A: 
- **Exam schedule, hall ticket, enrollment issues**: Exam Cell (examcell@kletu.ac.in)
- **Login, password, technical issues**: IT Support (support@kletu.ac.in)
- **Grade queries**: Course faculty or HOD
- **Account/profile issues**: Administration

**Q: Is there a phone number for emergencies?**
A: Check your institution's website or notice board for exam cell contact number. Use it only for urgent issues during exam season.

### What to Bring to Exam
✅ **Must Bring:**
- Hall ticket (printed)
- Student ID card
- Pens (blue/black ink)

✅ **Allowed (if needed):**
- Pencils and eraser
- Calculator (if exam allows)
- Transparent water bottle

❌ **NOT Allowed:**
- Mobile phones (must be switched off)
- Smartwatches
- Notes or textbooks
- Other electronic devices (unless specifically allowed)

### Exam Rules Summary
1. Arrive 30 minutes early
2. Sit at assigned seat only
3. Keep hall ticket visible on desk
4. No talking or looking at others' papers
5. Raise hand for permission (questions, restroom)
6. Stay seated until dismissed by invigilator
